Symmetry-Aware Parameter Inference for Physical Model Audio Synthesis

George Dangas, Alec Wright, Stefan Bilbao

This webpage accompanies the ICASSP 2027 submission Symmetry-aware Parameter Inference for Physical Model Audio Synthesis. It provides sound demos for the models discussed in the paper on a linear plate FDTD physical-model synthesizer.

Each reconstruction is generated by resynthesizing the plate model from parameters predicted by the corresponding inverse model. All samples are 5 s clips at 44.1 kHz and are peak-normalized for listening.

Abstract

Physical modeling audio synthesizers define forward maps from interpretable parameters to sound through a realized model description, dynamical state evolution, and readout. We study learning the inverse audio-to-parameter map with deep neural networks. This inverse can be non-unique: distinct parameter vectors can produce identical audio due to system-level symmetries such as reciprocity, geometry-induced symmetries such as reflection, and implementation-level effects introduced by discretization and excitation/readout operators. We formulate symmetry-aware physical-model inversion, instantiate it on a finite-difference time-domain (FDTD) rectangular linear plate scheme, and analyze symmetries of the implemented forward map. We evaluate orbit canonization, conditional flow matching (CFM) posterior modeling, and reciprocity-equivariant token CFM. Accounting for these symmetries substantially improves inverse learning.
